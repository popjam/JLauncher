# JLauncher

A minimal, text-only Android launcher with a mood and life tracker built in.

On top it's just your apps, the time and a few quiet numbers. Swipe left and there's a lot more:
daily check-ins, stats filled in by themselves (screen time, sleep, music, places, spending,
people, weather), insights about what goes with a better mood, a journal with voice notes, and a
report to bring to an appointment.

Everything stays on the phone. Backups go to a folder you pick.

## Building

`./gradlew assembleFast` builds the phone build; `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest` runs the tests.
